## Evidence labels

- `primary`: official documentation, original study, specification, or first-party report.
- `secondary`: independent summary or analysis.
- `vendor claim`: first-party effectiveness or performance claim without reproducible methodology.
- `unverified`: retained for investigation but not used to justify enforcement.
